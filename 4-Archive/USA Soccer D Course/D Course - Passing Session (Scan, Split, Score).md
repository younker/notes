# D Course - Passing Session: "Scan, Split, Score"

Coaching training session for the [[Coaching Certifications|USA Soccer D License]] activity design. Built for [[Boys Soccer Fall 26]] and tied to the team's current focus in [[Scanning and Decision Speed]]. Fills the [[2026-d-coaching-training-sessions-template-excel.xlsx|D Course template]].

## Session Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Age Group & Gender | High school boys, 11v11 grassroots (U15-U18) |
| Duration | 75-90 min session (this activity ~22 min) |
| Number of Players | 14-18 |
| Game Moment | Attacking (in possession) |
| Phase | Building/progressing through the middle third |
| Area of the Field | Middle third, central channel |

## Training Session Objective

Aligned with the 11v11 grassroots Age Group Learning Plan (tactical awareness, speed of play, combination play, playing out of pressure):

- Players keep possession and progress the ball forward through the middle third by playing quick, accurate passes and receiving with a directional first touch under pressure.
- Secondary: players scan before receiving and pre-select two options, so the decision is made before the ball arrives.

**Personal goal:** stay off the sideline megaphone. Coach in short moments and let the game teach (my own note from the [[11v11 Grassroots - Page of Experience|Page of Experience]] - "if I have to yell from the sideline, I didn't do my job in practice").

## Desired Behavior

- **Main Principle (attacking):** Possession with penetration - keep the ball to move it forward, not to keep it.
- **Sub Principle:** Support and mobility - create passing angles (triangles) and speed of play; receive on the half-turn.
- **Player Actions:**
  - Scan over the shoulder before the ball arrives.
  - Check to create an angle, then receive with an open body / hips pointed forward.
  - Directional first touch out of pressure into space.
  - Pass and move - support the next pass; don't admire it.
  - Play forward when it's on; recycle when it isn't.

## Coaching Points (age-appropriate language)

- "Check your shoulder before it comes - what's behind you?"
- "Two options before you get it. Plan A and plan B."
- "Open up. Point your hips where you want to go."
- "First touch out of your feet, into space."
- "Pass and move - don't fall in love with your pass."
- "Firm ball, back foot. Make it easy for him."
- "Fast and wrong beats slow and perfect." (decisiveness)

## ACTIVITY 1 - Directional Possession: "Scan, Split, Score"

**Activity Objective:** Keep the ball and progress it forward through pressure, using a pre-scan and a directional first touch to play into a target player in the end zone.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| # of players / Opponent | 6v6 + 2 neutrals (14). Scales to 5v5+2 or 7v7+2 |
| Size / Shape of Field | 40 x 30 yds, split in half; a 4-yd target end zone (full width) at each end |
| Ways of Scoring | 1 pt: complete a pass to a teammate who checks INTO the opponent's end zone and lays it back (received + returned). 2 pts: the entry pass is played first-time or off a directional first touch out of pressure. Neutrals play with whoever has the ball (always attack overload) but cannot score or enter end zones |
| Active / Recovery Duration | 4 min active / 90 sec recovery |
| Repetitions | 4 rounds |
| Total Activity Duration | ~22 min |
| Rules and restarts | Ball out = quick pass or dribble in from the side. Coach feeds a new ball instantly on any score or ball-out to hold tempo. Neutrals are always with the team in possession |
| Constraints / restrictions | Progression: 2-touch max, then 1-touch entry into the zone. Harder: point only counts if the receiver scanned (coach can freeze to check). Regression: unlimited touches; give neutrals free time on the ball |
| Rotations (substitutions) | Neutrals rotate every round so everyone gets central/high-value reps. Losing/resting team subs in each round |

**Why this activity:** the two end zones force forward progression (not sideways safety possession), the neutrals guarantee a support angle so players learn to find and use it, and the 2-pt bonus rewards exactly the scan + directional touch behavior. It's the SSG version of Week 1-3 in [[Scanning and Decision Speed]].

## Coaching Interactions

**WHAT (Activity 1 coaching points):** scan before receiving; open body / hips to target; directional first touch out of pressure; support angles and pass-and-move; recognize when to play forward vs. recycle.

**WHEN (I will influence the environment):** Play-Practice-Play - short moments, not constant stoppage.
- Let the first round run clean (the "play" - watch, don't talk).
- Individual whispers in the run of play to a shadowed player ("look before it comes").
- 1-2 quick group freezes per round, 5-10 sec max, at a clear teachable moment.
- Q&A at the 90-sec water breaks.

**HOW (I will deliver the information):**
- Guided discovery - ask, don't tell: "What did you see before it arrived? Where was your second option?"
- Quick demo only for a technical fix (body shape, touch direction).
- On-the-fly individual cues while shadowing a player.
- Praise decisive, quick play out loud; coach hesitation privately. Never punish a decisive wrong pass (Week 4 rule).
