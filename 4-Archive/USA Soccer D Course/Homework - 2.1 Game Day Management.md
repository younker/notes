## Team Playing Identity / Style of Play

How we want to play: possession-based, control the middle third, progress the ball with intentional passing and directional first touch. Avoid low-percentage long balls; build and probe for penetration into the final third.

## Objectives by Game Moment

| Game Moment | What we want to see |
|---|---|
| Attacking (in possession) | Keep the ball with purpose; scan before receiving; play forward when it's on, recycle when it isn't; create passing angles / triangles |
| Defending (out of possession) | Compact shape; pressure the ball, cover and balance behind; deny central penetration |
| Attacking Transition (won the ball) | First look forward; exploit space quickly before opponent resets |
| Defending Transition (lost the ball) | Immediate reaction - counter-press or recover into shape fast |
