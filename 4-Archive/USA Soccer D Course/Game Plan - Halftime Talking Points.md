## Planning Tab

**Describe the Game Context** *(age group, format, field, competition, weather, time of day)*

- MHSAA High school boys (11v11)
- Regular season match vs TC West at Petoskey HS Stadium (120x55 turf)
- Strong opponent; we expected fewer chances than usual

**What do your players need today to perform and learn?** *(developmental characteristics)*

Decisiveness and quick reads against a better team. Transition quickly through the middle (due to formation & available players for center/mid). Scan before receiving so you can know where you are going. Be patient, strike when opportunity presents and do not overcommit (they are very good at counter attacking)

**Main objectives for the team and/or individual players (and how they connect to recent training):**

1. Win the first five minutes: come out hard, surprise them with speed and power, control the ball and dictate the pace early
2. Make our chances count: Our chances will be fewer, so make each count: shots on frame, crash the net looking for bobbled saves.
3. Specific methods of attack:
  a. Left-side attack: Ashton hangs high, isolate him 1v1 on their right back and get a foot race. Three options off that race:  
      I. Ashton beats his man outside, drives near post
      II. Connor (our starting 9) crashes the six looking for deflections or simple pass from Ashton for tap in
      III. Roland (our starting 10) transition quickly to be at the 18 for a pass back from Ashton
  b. Right-side attack: release Noah overlapping the right midfielder for a numbers advantage. Westin should stay high to help keep his mark close allowing Noah freedom or forcing their D into a choice between the 1. Talk about how the back line shifts to cover the exposed right back should a quick counter happen when Noah is high
5. Backline: Has to step and to close the gap between midfield and back. We have lacked at this lately and they will absolutely use that space to counter.
6. Be smart with your energy! With so many kids hurt or out, we have to make the most of our runs

*Connects to recent training on speed of play, counter-pressing, and wide overloads.*


**What will you observe/pay attention to during the game to support learning:**

- Did we take come out hard in the first five minutes?
- How well are we controlling the ball transitioning into our middle third and, more importantly, our offensive third
- Can we lock the ball in our half for sustained periods of time
- How are they countering
- How are we attacking
- Are we making the most of our chances
- Are we staying calm but decisive on the ball (implies scanning)
- Where is their weak back (if not right back, can we pit Ashton against him?)
- Does the Noah/Westin overlap create the numbers advantage without exposing our right back?
- Is the gap between midfield and back line staying tight?
- Are we expending too much energy? Especially playing the long ball too frequently
