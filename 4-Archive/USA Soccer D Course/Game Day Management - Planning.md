## Planning

### 1. Game context
> Age group, game format, field, competition, weather, time of day

High school boys varsity match, 11v11, grass field, dry conditions 4PM

### 2. What players need today to perform and learn
> Consider the developmental characteristics

It a wide range for teenage players with the older players dramatically more developed than other peers. Many have a strong desire to improve themselves. For a new coach, they also have a need to impress the coach. For many boys there is alot of pressure to make varsity for the first time. many of these boys are horomone driven and impulsive in the heat of competition so we need to focus on preparing them beforehand to make good decisions quickly in the heat of the game.

They all need to have fun and find joy in pushing themself and in competition without it impacting their sense of self.

Our season theme is "direction" so the main thing is they need to determine what direction they want soccer to take them.

### 3. Main objectives, and how they connect to your most recent training
> For the team and/or individual players

Our most recent training was a combination of:
- awareness and use of (north/south) half spaces
- increase rate of controlled entrance into our offensive 1/3
- we are also always working on scanning before receiving the ball to create a plan and backup option


### 4. What I'll observe during the game to support player learning

- I am watching how we transition through our middle third
- Are we finding feet (aka do we have a plan before we get the ball) or panicing and kicking the ball
- What parts of the game do we consistently execute controlled entry vs long ball and who are the players initiating these decisions
- I'm always trying to read body language for mental or physical fatigue 
- Trying to keep a list of the top 2 or 3 things (max) that I am seeing which we need to celebrate and/or improve
