# Record-Eagle prep roundup - email template

## Shell

```
To: sports@record-eagle.com
Subject: <Sport>: Petoskey <result> <opponent> <M/D>

<Headline>
<Lead paragraph: who won, score, opponent, context.>
"<Coach quote, 1-2 sentences.>" Petoskey coach Jason Younker said.
Northmen leaders: <name stat, stat; name stat; keeper name saves (shutout)>.
```

## Fields

- **Headline** - subject + verb + result. "Petoskey edges Cadillac", "Cadillac
  tops Petoskey", "Petoskey, Gaylord play to draw".
- **Lead** - final score, opponent, home/away, record after, any context.
- **Quote** - real quote from the coach only. Ask for it; never invent.
- **Leaders** - scorers (goals/assists), goalkeeper saves, shutout if kept.

## Worked example (soccer)

```
To: sports@record-eagle.com
Subject: Boys soccer: Petoskey def. Cadillac 3-1, 8/26

Petoskey opens with home win over Cadillac
Petoskey opened its season with a 3-1 home win over Cadillac on Tuesday,
scoring twice in the second half to pull away. The Northmen improve to 1-0.
"The guys stayed patient and took their chances when they came," Petoskey
coach Jason Younker said. "A good first step for a young group."
Northmen leaders: Sam Rue 2 goals, 1 assist; Eli Dettmer 1 goal; keeper Jack
Poe 6 saves.
```
