# Scanning and Decision Speed

Summary of "Your Brain Is Slow On The Ball — Here's How To Fix It" (Football Ascend, ~7 min). Training plan below is for [[Boys Soccer Fall 26]].

## Core idea

Slow play is perceptual lag, not poor technique. With 0.5-1.5 seconds on the ball in tight spaces, the decision must happen before the ball arrives; the touch only executes a thought already made. Repetition alone doesn't fix it — perception has to be trained deliberately.

## The 4 laws

1. **Scan before you need to.** Proactive scanning off the ball, constantly updating a map of the pitch. Habit: "if the ball came to me now, what would I do?"
2. **Reduce options before receiving.** Pre-decide two options: first choice plus backup. Receiving is confirming, not deciding.
3. **Use the first touch to buy time.** Directional touch away from pressure into space, set up so it starts the pre-decided action.
4. **Wrong decisions are part of right thinking.** Fast good-enough beats slow perfect. Hesitation is its own mistake; decisive errors are recoverable.

Film habit: pause footage before a player receives, guess the choice, compare. 10 min/day.

## Training plan

Layer onto existing sessions, one law per week, then keep all four as standing cues. Freeze-frame question all month: "what did you see before it arrived?"

**Week 1 — Scanning**
- Rondo: mandatory shoulder check before every reception. Random freeze: "what's behind you?"
- SSG rule: off-ball players call their next action before the ball arrives.

**Week 2 — Two options**
- Possession game: on freeze, receiver must name plan A and plan B. No plan B, ball turns over.
- 4v4+3: coach calls a color mid-pass; that option is closed, receiver plays the backup.

**Week 3 — First touch**
- Receiving patterns: directional touch away from a presser, passive then live.
- SSG scoring: goals built on one- and two-touch play count double.

**Week 4 — Decisiveness**
- Shot-clock possession: 2 seconds max on the ball.
- Coaching rule: praise fast-but-wrong, coach hesitation. Never punish a decisive mistake.

**Ongoing**
- Film sessions (bus, team dinners): pause-before-receive, players vote on the choice.
- Match cue: between touches, work with your eyes, not your feet.
