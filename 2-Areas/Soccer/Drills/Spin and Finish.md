# Spin and Finish

Diagram: [Spin and Finish.html](Spin%20and%20Finish.html)

![[Spin and Finish.png|400]]

Original sketch: ![[Spin and Finish - sketch.jpg|300]]

## Setup

Goal at one end. Three cones in a triangle in front of goal. X3 beside the top-left cone. X1, X4 and X2 in a line at the back, X4 in the middle.

## Sequence

1. X1 passes to X4.
2. X4 passes back to X1.
3. X4 spins.
4. X2 passes to X4's back foot.
5. X4 takes a back-foot set-up touch.
6. X4 passes to X3.
7. X3 passes back to X4.
8. X4 moves onto the return.
9. X4 shoots.

## Coaching points

- Step, check, plant, finish. As you step, check the keeper's position, so that as you plant you can finish with a placed shot.
