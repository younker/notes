# Numbers Call

Diagram: [Numbers Call.html](Numbers%20Call.html)

![[Numbers Call.png|400]]

Original sketch: ![[Numbers Call - sketch.jpg|300]]

## Setup

Half field: the goal with a GK, the 18 and the halfway line. O1, O2, O3 and O4 defend in a back four just above the 18. X1, X2, X3 and X4 start spread across the field around midfield, each with a ball. The coach stands near midfield.

## Part 1: 1v4

1. X1, X2, X3 and X4 dribble toward the defense.
2. The coach calls a number from 1 to 4 (here, 3). X3 dribbles at goal.
3. X1, X2 and X4 peel off with their balls.
4. O1, O2, O3 and O4 shift toward X3 and find their shape.
5. X3 shoots. Once the ball is shot or out of bounds, all four restart up top. Run 5 reps.

## Part 2: 2v4, building to 4v4

1. X1, X2, X3 and X4 dribble toward the defense.
2. The coach calls two numbers (here, 3 then 2). X3, the first number, keeps the ball and dribbles at goal.
3. X2, the second number, leaves their ball and runs to support X3.
4. X1 and X4 peel off with their balls.
5. O1, O2, O3 and O4 shift toward the ball and find their shape against the 2v4.
6. X3 and X2 attack 2v4 and shoot. Once the ball is shot or out of bounds, all four restart up top. After 5 reps, the coach calls three numbers for 3v4, then four for 4v4. The first number keeps the ball and the others leave theirs and support.

## Coaching points

- The defense adjusts to the called player and finds its shape.
- The back four shift together toward the ball.
