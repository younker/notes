# Race to the Ball

Diagram: [Race to the Ball.html](Race%20to%20the%20Ball.html)

![[Race to the Ball.png|400]]

Original sketch: ![[Race to the Ball - sketch.jpg|300]]

## Setup

Two gates of two cones each at the top, one left and one right. X1 and X2 each start at a cone at the bottom, with a second cone straight ahead of them. X3 stands at the top between the gates with the balls.

## Sequence

1. X1 and X2 run to their first cone.
2. X1 and X2 run back to their start cone.
3. X3 passes into the middle.
4. X1 and X2 race to the ball.
5. The player who wins the ball dribbles through either gate.
