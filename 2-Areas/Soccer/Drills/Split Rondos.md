# Split Rondos

Diagram: [Split Rondos.html](Split%20Rondos.html)

![[Split Rondos.png|400]]

## Setup

Three 12x12 yard grids, each with a cone on every corner. Six players per grid: four Xs, one on each side of the grid, and two Os inside. All 18 players work, GKs included. The diagram shows one grid.

Eight passes in a row scores 1 point. A split pass between the two Os scores 2. When an O wins the ball, that O swaps with the X who lost it.

Play 4 rounds of 3 minutes with 45 seconds rest. Rounds 3 and 4 are 2-touch max.

## Sequence

1. X1 passes to X2.
2. X4 checks along the side of the grid to open a lane between O1 and O2.
3. X2 passes to X4 between O1 and O2. A split pass scores 2 points.

## Coaching points

- Check your shoulder before the ball arrives. Know where the split lane is.
- Open your body. Point your hips where you want to play.
- Move along your side to make the angle. Don't stand still.
- Firm pass through the middle. Watch the weight, not just the direction.
