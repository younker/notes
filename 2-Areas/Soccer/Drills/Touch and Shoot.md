# Touch and Shoot

Diagram: [Touch and Shoot.html](Touch%20and%20Shoot.html)

![[Touch and Shoot.png|400]]

Original sketch: ![[Touch and Shoot - sketch.jpg|300]]

## Setup

Goal at one end. Three cones in a triangle out from the goal. X2 wide of the triangle, X1 behind X2.

## Sequence

1. X2 passes to X1.
2. X1 one-touches it back to X2.
3. X1 runs around the outside of the near cone into the triangle.
4. X2 passes through the cones to X1.
5. X1 takes a set-up touch out through the far side of the triangle.
6. X1 shoots on goal.
