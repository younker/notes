Setup

A 45x35 yard field in the middle third with a 4-yard end zone across each end, marked with cones. Xs attack the top end zone and Os attack the bottom. Seven Xs, seven Os, and two jokers (J1, J2) who play with whichever team has the ball, so the team in possession is always 9v7. Jokers can't score or enter an end zone. The GKs work separately.

A point is a completed pass to a teammate who checks into the end zone and lays it back. It's 2 points if the entry pass is first-time or comes right after a directional first touch.

Rounds are 4 minutes or first to 5 points, with 60 seconds between rounds. Play 3 rounds and swap the jokers each round. A ball out of play restarts with the other team from their own end zone. Progression: 2-touch max.

Sequence

  1. X1 passes to X3.
  2. X3 takes a directional first touch forward into space.
  3. X7 checks into the end zone.
  4. X3 passes to X7 in the end zone, between O5 and O7.
  5. X7 passes back to X6. The entry came off a directional first touch, so it scores 2 points.

Coaching points

  • Check your shoulder before the ball arrives.
  • Have two options before you receive. Decide before the ball gets there.
  • Open your hips toward where your first touch is going.
  • First touch out of your feet and into space.
  • Pass and move.
  • Watch the weight of the pass, not just the direction.
  • Be decisive. Fast and wrong beats slow and perfect.
