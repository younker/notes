# Speed Ks

Diagram: [Speed Ks.html](Speed%20Ks.html)

![[Speed Ks.png|400]]

## Setup

Goal with a GK and a ball on the penalty spot. The rest of the team lines up behind the spot, outside the 18, each with a ball. The diagram shows X1 on the spot and X2, X3 and X4 in line.

## Sequence

1. X1 shoots from the penalty spot.
2. X1 runs to the goal and becomes the GK.
3. The GK rotates to the back of the line.
4. X2 places a ball on the spot and shoots as soon as X1 is set in goal.

## Coaching points

- Keep the line moving. The goal is as many PKs per player as possible.
- Track each player's makes to help set the PK order.
