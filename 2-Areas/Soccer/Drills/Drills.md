# Drills

Drill library for building practice plans. Each drill has a note, an HTML diagram, and a PNG thumbnail.

![[Drills.base]]

## Passing

- [[Pass and Touch Cones]] - 4 players, two triangles, pass through a middle gate and rotate
- [[Square Lay-off]] - 8 players, long pass up each side of a cone square, receiver lays it off short to the corner player who plays the next side

## Possession

- [[Split Rondos]] - 18 players, three 12x12 grids of 4v2, 8 passes for 1 point, a split pass between the defenders for 2
- [[Scan Split Score]] - 16 players, 7v7 + 2 jokers to end zones, score by checking into the zone and laying it back, 2 points off a directional first touch
- [[Through the Middle]] - 18 players, GK + 8v8 + GK in thirds, the ball must be received in the middle third, split-pass goals count double

## Transition

- [[Build Out to Target]] - 8 players, GK + 3 build out past 3 to a target player (T), who switches the ball to the 3 to counter on the big goal

## Finishing

- [[Touch and Shoot]] - 2 players, one-touch return, run into a cone triangle, set-up touch and shot
- [[Three Finishes]] - 2 players, three back-to-back patterns (pass, give and go around a cone, cross), each ending in a shot
- [[Spin and Finish]] - 4 players, give and go, spin, back-foot setup, wall pass with X3, shot
- [[Rapid Fire 3v2]] - 6 players plus GK and server, pass and shoot, then 2v1, then 3v2 in rapid waves; defenders counter into mini goals
- [[Speed Ks]] - whole team, rapid-fire PKs: shoot, go in goal, rotate to the back of the line

## 1v1

- [[Race to the Ball]] - 3 players, X1 and X2 race out and back, then race to X3's pass and dribble through a gate
- [[1v1 Gates]] - 2 players, X1 serves to X2 in a corner and closes down, X2 dribbles through either gate
- [[1v1 Agility Shot]] - 2 players, weave around cones and race to a ball, winner shoots, loser brings a second ball in for a 1v1

## Small-sided Games

- [[3v3v3 Three Goals]] - 9 players, three teams of 3 each guard a goal and score on either of the other two; 90-second games, first to 3, or lose a player when scored on

## Defending

- [[Numbers Call]] - 9 players, 4 Xs dribble at a back four, the coach calls who attacks, building from 1v4 to 4v4 while the defense shifts and finds its shape
