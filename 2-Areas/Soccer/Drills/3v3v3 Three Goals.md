# 3v3v3 Three Goals

Diagram: [3v3v3 Three Goals.html](3v3v3%20Three%20Goals.html)

![[3v3v3 Three Goals.png|400]]

Original sketch: ![[3v3v3 Three Goals - sketch.jpg|300]]

## Setup

Three goals in a triangle: two near the top corners, angled toward the middle, and one centered at the bottom. Three teams of three: A1, A2 and A3 guard the top-left goal, O1, O2 and O3 guard the top-right goal, and X1, X2 and X3 guard the bottom goal. Each team defends its own goal and can score on either of the other two.

Play one of three formats: 90-second games, first to 3 goals, or every time a team is scored on it loses a player.

## Sequence

1. A1 passes to A2.
2. A2 dribbles into the middle.
3. A2 shoots on the O goal or the X goal.
