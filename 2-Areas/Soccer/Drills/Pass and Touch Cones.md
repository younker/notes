# Pass and Touch Cones

Diagram: [Pass and Touch Cones.html](Pass%20and%20Touch%20Cones.html)

![[Pass and Touch Cones.png|400]]

Original sketch: ![[Pass and Touch Cones - sketch.jpg|300]]

## Setup

Two triangles of cones facing each other across a two-cone gate in the middle. Two players per triangle: one in the middle, one at the back cone. X1/X2 bottom, X3/X4 top.

## Sequence

1. X1 passes through the gate to X3. X1 runs to touch a side cone, then goes to the back cone. X2 leaves the back cone, touches the other side cone and sets up in the middle.
2. X3 passes to X2 and does the same run (side cone, back cone) while X4 rotates into the top middle.
3. X2 passes to X4, then runs to the back cone as X1 rotates back into the middle.
4. X4 passes to X1, then runs to the back cone as X3 rotates into the middle.
5. Repeat.

Pass order: X1 > X3 > X2 > X4 > X1.
