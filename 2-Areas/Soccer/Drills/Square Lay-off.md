# Square Lay-off

Diagram: [Square Lay-off.html](Square%20Lay-off.html)

![[Square Lay-off.png|400]]

Original sketch: ![[Square Lay-off - sketch.jpg|300]] ![[Square Lay-off - sketch 2.jpg|300]]

## Setup

Four cones in a square. A corner player stands just outside each corner: X1 bottom right, X3 top right, X5 top left, X7 bottom left. A receiver stands past each corner on the extension of the side leading into it: X2 above the top-right corner, X4 left of the top-left corner, X6 below the bottom-left corner, X8 right of the bottom-right corner. X1 starts with the ball.

## Sequence

1. X1 passes to X2 up the right side.
2. X2 passes short to X3 at the corner.
3. X3 passes to X4 along the top.
4. X4 passes short to X5 at the corner.
5. X5 passes to X6 down the left side.
6. X6 passes short to X7 at the corner.
7. X7 passes to X8 along the bottom.
8. X8 passes short to X1 at the corner, and the pattern repeats.

## Coaching points

- After four laps, switch direction.
