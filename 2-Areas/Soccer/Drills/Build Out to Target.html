Build Out to Target

Setup

Ball starts with Keeper. Xs build out of the back trying to get the ball to T (target) at the top of the grid. Os lay defense.

If Os win the ball, they immediately counter on goal.

If Xs get to T, then play quickly shifts and T plays to Os for a counter attack. X quickly reshapes into defensive structure.

After every shot or ball out of play, the GK restarts by passing to the Xs.

Offside applies when the Os attack the big goal, with the offside line at the edge of the 18.

Sequence

  1. The GK passes to X3.
  2. X3 passes to T, past O1, O2 and O3.
  3. T passes to O1. Direction changes as soon as T receives.
  4. O1 shoots on the big goal.
