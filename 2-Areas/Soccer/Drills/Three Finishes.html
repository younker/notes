Three Finishes

Three short patterns run back to back, each ending in a shot. Goal at one end. Three cones: X2's start cone, a middle cone, and a far cone. X1 serves from behind.

Part 1: pass, touch, shoot

  1. X1 passes to X2.
  2. X2 takes a set-up touch.
  3. X2 shoots.

Part 2: give and go, run around the cone

  1. X2 starts between the two left cones, where they took the first shot, and checks back to X1.
  2. Give and go: X1 passes to X2, X2 passes back to X1.
  3. X2 runs around the middle cone on the side away from goal.
  4. X1 passes to X2 on the goal side of the cone. X2 receives on the inside foot.
  5. X2 takes a set-up touch.
  6. X2 shoots.

Part 3: cross from X1

  1. After shooting, X2 runs around the far cone.
  2. While X2 is rounding the cone, X1 crosses the ball.
  3. X2 takes a set-up touch.
  4. X2 shoots.
