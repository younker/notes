Through the Middle

Setup

A 70x50 yard field with a full-size goal and a GK at each end, split into thirds with cones. Eight Xs attack the top goal and eight Os attack the bottom.

Before the ball can enter the attacking third, a player has to receive it in the middle third and play it on. A long ball over the middle is a turnover. A goal that comes from a split pass or a directional first touch in the middle third counts double.

Play 3 blocks of 7 minutes with 90 seconds rest. The last block is free play with no conditions. GKs restart with a pass or throw.

Sequence

  1. X3 passes to X5 in the middle third.
  2. X5 takes a directional first touch forward.
  3. X8 runs in behind, between O2 and O3.
  4. X5 passes to X8 between O2 and O3.
  5. X8 shoots. The goal came from a split pass, so it counts double.

Coaching points

  • Find the player between the lines. Don't skip the middle.
  • Play forward when it's on, recycle when it isn't.
  • Time the run in behind with the receiver's first touch.
  • Same cues as Scan Split Score: scan, open hips, directional first touch.
