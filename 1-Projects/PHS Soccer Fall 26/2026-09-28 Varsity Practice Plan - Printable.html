1. Pass and Touch Cones

Time: ______ min

Setup. Two cone triangles facing each other across a middle gate. Two players per triangle: one in the middle, one at the back cone.

  1. X1 passes through the gate to X3, runs to touch a side cone, then goes to the back cone. X2 touches the other side cone and sets up in the middle.
  2. X3 passes to X2 and does the same run while X4 rotates into the top middle.
  3. X2 passes to X4, then runs to the back cone as X1 rotates into the middle.
  4. X4 passes to X1 and runs as X3 rotates in.
  5. Repeat. Pass order: X1 > X3 > X2 > X4 > X1.

2. Touch and Shoot

Time: ______ min

Setup. Goal at one end. Three cones in a triangle out from the goal. X2 wide of the triangle, X1 behind X2.

  1. X2 passes to X1.
  2. X1 one-touches it back to X2.
  3. X1 runs around the outside of the near cone into the triangle.
  4. X2 passes through the cones to X1.
  5. X1 takes a set-up touch out through the far side of the triangle.
  6. X1 shoots.

3. Three Finishes

Time: ______ min

Setup. Goal at one end. X2's start cone on the left, a middle cone, a far cone on the right. X1 serves from behind. Three patterns back to back, each ending in a shot.

Part 1: pass, touch, shoot

  1. X1 passes to X2.
  2. X2 set-up touch.
  3. X2 shoots.

Part 2: give and go, around the cone

  1. X2 checks back to X1.
  2. Give and go: X1 passes to X2, X2 passes back.
  3. X2 runs around the middle cone, away from goal.
  4. X1 passes to X2 on the goal side of the cone, inside foot.
  5. X2 set-up touch.
  6. X2 shoots.

Part 3: cross

  1. X2 runs around the far cone.
  2. X1 crosses the ball.
  3. X2 set-up touch.
  4. X2 shoots.

4. Spin and Finish

Time: ______ min

Setup. Goal at one end. Three cones in a triangle in front of goal. X3 beside the top-left cone. X1, X4 and X2 in a line at the back.

  1. X1 passes to X4.
  2. X4 passes back to X1.
  3. X4 spins.
  4. X2 passes to X4's back foot.
  5. X4 back-foot set-up touch.
  6. X4 passes to X3.
  7. X3 passes back to X4.
  8. X4 moves onto the return.
  9. X4 shoots.

Coaching point. Step, check, plant, finish. As you step, check the keeper's position, so that as you plant you can finish with a placed shot.
