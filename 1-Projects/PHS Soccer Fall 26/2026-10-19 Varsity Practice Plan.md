# 2026-10-19 Varsity Practice Plan

Focus: Small-sided play, transition and finishing; PK rotation for districts

## Description

Review. With a big game coming up, we need to go over things we have been working on all season. Working in possession in small then medium spaces followed by finishing exercises. For the control section, focus on players scanning and having a plan prior to receiving the ball. Play forward through pressure when possible, recycle when it's not, (counter) attack quickly when on turnovers and recognizing when not to force it if the lane closes. End with PKs to prep for such an outcome with the goal being a refined list of who our first and second 5 are.
