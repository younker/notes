Point: Cones 25 yards apart. Each team kicks for the cone; closest ball wins the point. If tied, the two players who trapped the balls pass to the far cone; closest wins. Repeat until the point is decided. Home kicks first, then the winner of each point kicks first on the next.
Game: First to 5 points. Match: Best of 3 games. Game 3 is played at 30 yards.
Seeding: 1) win %, 2) head-to-head, 3) game difference per match, 4) one sudden-death point at 25 yards.
Pairs 5 and 10 cannot finish above .500 and do not advance.
