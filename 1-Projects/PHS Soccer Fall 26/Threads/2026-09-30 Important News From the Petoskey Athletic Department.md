# Important News From the Petoskey Athletic Department

**1 message** on 2026-09-30, posted by AD [[Mike Bakker]] (ParentSquare, Petoskey Middle School).

## Key info

- **New athletics website:** https://www.petoskeyathletics.org/ - contest schedules tied to the new scheduling platform, updated in real time. Will also carry articles, social posts, and sponsor recognition.
- **FinalForms** is now the athletic registration system, effective immediately.
  - Every family with a current or prospective student-athlete must create an account ASAP (10-15 min). Interscholastic only, not intramurals.
  - Coaches get direct access to their own athletes' emergency medical info, parent contacts, and health history.
  - Families upload signed physicals annually. A 2026-27 physical already turned in does not need re-upload.
  - Students and parents e-sign forms annually.
  - **Replaces the "Ok to Play" cards.**
- Linked in the original (URLs not captured): FinalForms Introduction Letter, Parent Playbook Info, FinalForms login page (also linked from the new athletics site).

## 1. 2026-09-30 08:51 - Mike Bakker

Hello Northmen,

Happy Homecoming Week!

The Athletic Department has exciting news about two important updates we have been working on since July 2026.

First, our scheduling platforms have changed a lot over the past 12 months, which has also affected our athletic website. We are pleased to announce that we have transitioned to a new platform, which has also allowed us to reconnect and update our athletic website. You can now find contest schedules that are directly tied to our scheduling platform and updated in real time. This new platform will also allow us to post articles, create social media posts, and recognize our athletic sponsors. You will see incremental updates as we become more familiar with the platform and its tools. Be sure to bookmark this URL for our newly updated website: https://www.petoskeyathletics.org/

The second big announcement is a new student management system that we will start using immediately for athletic registration. This new platform, FinalForms, is a leader in educational athletics and will help our athletic department communicate in and out of season with coaches, parents, and student-athletes. It will provide coaches with immediate access to important information about their athletes only, including emergency medical information, parent contact information, and health history. As a department, we will also be able to track participation history, physicals, awards, equipment, initial academic eligibility, and more. I have attached links to a parent letter about FinalForms, a parent guide on how to use FinalForms, and our Petoskey FinalForms site. All families with a current student-athlete, or one planning to try out for a sport, will be required to create an account. (Note: this is for participation in interscholastic athletics, not intramural sport teams at this time.) We ask that you do this as soon as possible. It only takes about 10 - 15 minutes to create your account and select the sports you want to participate in. If you have multiple children who participate in athletics, you can copy most of your parent information to each new child without having to re-enter the information. You can update sports and student information anytime. Once you've created an account, each year you will just update/verify your information and sign that year's forms. This new platform will also allow families to upload their signed physicals annually. If you have turned in a 2026-27 school year physical, you do not need to upload your physical this year. Once your account is created, students and parents must also electronically sign the forms annually.

One bonus for students and coaches is that FinalForms will replace the "Ok to Play" cards.

Here are the links you will need:
FinalForms Introduction Letter
Parent Playbook Info
FinalForms Login page (there is also a link to the login page on the new athletic website)

Please reach out if you have any questions about these new resources or anything else regarding the Athletic Department.

Go NORTHMEN!

Yours in Educational Athletics,
Mike Bakker, CMAA
Athletic Director
Public Schools of Petoskey
