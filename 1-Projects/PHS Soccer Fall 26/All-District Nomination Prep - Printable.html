Talking points

  • Attie: 15 G, 39% of team goals. 13 in BNC play, 8 vs District 17. Scored in 11 of 21 games including vs both TC schools.
  • McLintock: 11 A plus 6 G on a 38-goal team. 9 assists in BNC. A point in 7 of 12 BNC games. 2 pks.
  • O'Donnell: 4 G, 5 A. 2 G + 2 A against Alpena and Gaylord, the district rivals that matter.
  • Melvin: all 6 pts in conference, 5 vs D17. Converted every pk (Sault, Alpena).
  • Stradling: 7 clean sheets: Grass Lake, Cadillac x2, Sault x2, Gaylord, Alpena. 4 of them vs District 17 teams.
  • Team: 7-1 vs district field, 19-5 GF-GA. Only district team besides Marquette with a winning record. Beat Alpena 3 times (3-2, 3-0, 2-1 OT) and Gaylord twice. Lost the district final 0-1 to a 12-6-3 Marquette side that beat Gaylord 7-1.
  • Schedule: 4 games vs TC West (17-2-3) and TC Central (13-2-2), who went 19-0-5 in BNC play. Non-conf: Haslett, Boyne Mtn Invitational, Midland (W 2-1).
  • Coach of the Year counter: 8-4 BNC, 3rd behind two of the top D1 teams in the state. Hill likely gets it as district champ.

Before the vote

  • Nominees and stats must be entered at coach.mihssca.com before the meeting. Enter everyone with a case. Nothing can be added mid-meeting.
  • GK and D are voted first. Have minutes and games played for the back line.
  • Criteria MIHSSCA lists: stats, team success, strength of schedule, postseason, previous awards, head-to-head, common opponents, games played, college interest, club.
  • Only the 3 GK, 5 D, 12 M/F and COY advance. The 12 at-large do not. Argue ranking order, not just inclusion.
