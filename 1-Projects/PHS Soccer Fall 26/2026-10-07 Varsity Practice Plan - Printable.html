Focus: 1v1 and finishing

1. Rapid Fire 3v2

Time: ______ min

Part 1: pass and shoot

  1. The server passes to X1 inside the penalty arc.
  2. X1 shoots from the arc. If the ball stays in play, X1 goes for the rebound. When the ball is out of play or the GK has it, Part 2 starts.

Part 2: 2v1

  1. X2 dribbles into the 18 from the side, between the mini goal and the end line, with the second ball.
  2. O1 enters beside X2 and defends.
  3. X1 and X2 attack 2v1 and shoot. If O1 wins the ball, O1 scores on a mini goal. When the ball is out of play or the GK has it, Part 3 starts.

Part 3: 3v2

  1. X3 dribbles into the 18 from the other side, between the mini goal and the end line, with the third ball.
  2. O2 enters beside X3 and defends.
  3. X1, X2 and X3 attack 3v2 and shoot. If the Os win the ball, they score on a mini goal. When the ball is out of play or the GK has it, the rep ends and the next group starts Part 1.

Coaching points

  • Keep it rapid: the next pair enters the moment the ball is out of play or the GK has it.
  • Xs chase every rebound that stays in play.
  • Os who win the ball go straight to a mini goal; the Xs must recover and defend.

3. 1v1 Agility Shot

Time: ______ min
  1. X1 and X2 weave around their two cones and race to ball 1.
  2. The player who wins the race takes a set-up touch and shoots.
  3. The player who lost the race runs to ball 2.
  4. They dribble ball 2 at goal while the winner recovers and plays defense, 1v1 to finish.

4. Touch and Shoot

Time: ______ min
  1. X2 passes to X1.
  2. X1 one-touches it back to X2.
  3. X1 runs around the outside of the near cone into the triangle.
  4. X2 passes through the cones to X1.
  5. X1 takes a set-up touch out through the far side of the triangle.
  6. X1 shoots on goal.
