# Notes for Next Season

Running list of things to carry into Fall 27. See [[Program Context]], [[Boys Soccer Fall 26]], and the task plan in [[Fall 27 Season Plan]].

## Tryouts are too short to get the varsity squad right

Three days of tryouts is not enough to sort a varsity squad, especially with the skill spread on this roster. Some picks will be wrong. Say that out loud at the [[2026-08-12 Parent Meeting]] and at the first player meeting so nobody treats the initial [[Master Roster]] split as a verdict.

Expect fluidity between JV and varsity all season. Players move both directions, and that is the system working, not a punishment.

### Bubble players develop on both teams

Kids on the bubble play JV and varsity:

- JV for minutes - real game reps, on the field, making decisions under pressure.
- Varsity for exposure - training speed, the pace of the game, and time around the varsity group.

Neither alone develops a bubble player. Minutes without exposure caps their ceiling; exposure without minutes leaves them watching. Coordinate with [[Matt Hill]] on who is dual-rostered each week so the JV lineup isn't getting gutted on game day.
