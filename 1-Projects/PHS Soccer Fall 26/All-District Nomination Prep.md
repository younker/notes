# All-District Nomination Prep

Material for the All-District team meeting: records of every Big North Conference (BNC) team plus Marquette, Petoskey's results and scorers against each of them, and common-opponent comparisons. Team records and scores are from MHSAA (my.mhsaa.com schedule endpoint, pulled 2026-10-25). Petoskey player stats are from [[Season Stats]] (the coaching spreadsheet). Roster details in [[Master Roster]].

**District 17 field** (the coaches who vote): Marquette, Petoskey, Gaylord, Alpena, Sault Ste Marie. Bracket results: Gaylord 3-0 Sault (Oct 14), Petoskey 2-1 Alpena OT (Oct 20), Marquette 7-1 Gaylord (Oct 20), Marquette 1-0 Petoskey (Oct 22). Marquette plays Cedar Springs in Regional 5 on Oct 27.

MHSAA lists Alpena's Sept 19 Cheboygan 5-0 win twice; the duplicate is removed, which takes Alpena from 10-11-1 and 58 GF to 9-11-1 and 53 GF (GA unchanged). Field average is 2.48 GF and 2.30 GA per game across the eight teams. **GF vs field** is a team's goals per game relative to that average (positive is better). **GA vs field** is the same for goals allowed (negative is better). **GF share** is GF / (GF + GA), 50% means even.

- **Attie**: 15 goals is 39% of the team's 38. 13 of them came in conference play, and 8 against District 17 teams. Scored in 11 of 21 games, including against both TC schools.
- **McLintock**: 11 assists on a 38-goal team, 9 in conference. Had a point in 7 of the 12 BNC games.
