## Remaining Pool Play

Shortened to start the bracket sooner. Two matches, played at the same time:

- Match 11: 3 W. Giffin / Webster v 8 Steffes / Retherford - winner takes the last bracket spot
- Match 12: 4 Werth / Bolin v 5 Wong / A. Giffin - sets pair 4's seed

Pairs 5 and 10 cannot finish above .500 and do not advance.

Seeding: 1) win %, 2) head-to-head, 3) game difference per match, 4) one sudden-death point at 25 yards.

## Bracket

Top 8 by seeding, NCAA format. Higher seed is home.
- QF: Match 13 (1 v 8), Match 14 (4 v 5), Match 15 (2 v 7), Match 16 (3 v 6)
- SF: Match 17 (W13 v W14), Match 18 (W15 v W16)
- Match 19: 3rd place (L17 v L18)
- Match 20: Final (W17 v W18)
