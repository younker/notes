## What to expect (researched 2026-10-25)

From the MIHSSCA All-State Process page (https://mihssca.com/general-information/all-state-process/) and membership page. MIHSSCA publishes no procedure for how the meeting itself is run, so the mechanics below are standard practice, not written rules.

**Eligibility gates**
- Coach must be a paid MIHSSCA member for this team ($60, $90 after Oct 8) with the team added for the fall season.
- Nominees and their stats entered at coach.mihssca.com before the meeting. Not entered = not eligible, for All-District and everything above it.
- Players with 5 or fewer games before the MHSAA tournament should not be nominated.
- If the head coach cannot attend, another registered staff coach may.

**Structure (32 players + coach)**
- District Coach of the Year, then 3 GK, 5 defenders, 12 midfield/forward, then 12 at-large of any position.
- Advance to All-Region: the 3 GK, 5 D, 12 mid/fwd, and the District COY. The 12 at-large do not advance.
- All-Region is picked by the four district chairs (Hill represents District 17), not by all coaches: 3 GK, 5 D, 22 mid/fwd, plus 10 honorable mention in D2. All-Region is at least All-State Honorable Mention.
- No requirement that every team be represented.

**Listed criteria**: season stats, team success, strength of schedule, postseason success, previous awards, head-to-head, results against common opponents, games played, college interest, club affiliation.

**How it usually runs**: chair shares the registered nominee list; each coach speaks briefly for their players; vote position by position, usually by consensus or a quick ranked vote; coaches typically abstain on their own players. With 5 teams and 32 spots, most legitimate nominees get on; the fight is over the 20 advancing spots and the ordering of the 5 D and 12 mid/fwd, since ranking carries to region.
