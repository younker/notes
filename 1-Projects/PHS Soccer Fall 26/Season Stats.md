## Flags

Issues in the spreadsheet, not corrected here:

- **Aug 25 at Gaylord score.** Spreadsheet score cell says Gaylord 2, Petoskey 1 (a loss), but the same row credits two Petoskey goals (Duch, O'Donnell pk). MHSAA has it as a 2-1 Petoskey win. The game log above uses the MHSAA score. With that fix the log matches the 12-8-1 record on the spreadsheet header and MHSAA.
- **Conference record.** Spreadsheet header says 9-5-1. MHSAA says 8-4 over 12 Big North games (Sault is in the conference; Glen Lake, Harbor Springs, Midland and the invitational are not). Nothing in the 12 league games was a tie, so the spreadsheet figure is wrong.
- **Oct 12 at Alpena missing a goal.** MHSAA score is 3-0 but the spreadsheet lists only two scorers (Melvin pk, Attie). One goal is unattributed, so the leaders table is one goal short of the team's 38.
- **Stray MHSAA entry.** MHSAA lists a Tue Oct 27, 6:00 PM home contest at Click Rd. Soccer Complex with no opponent or score. Looks like an unused district-final placeholder; the season ended Oct 22.
- **Game 15 date.** Spreadsheet says 09.25.26 for the away game at Soo. The [[Official PHS Soccer Schedule]] and [[2026-09-29 vs Soo - Varsity Breakdown.pdf]] have it on Tue Sept 29. Corrected to Sept 29 above.
- **Duch spelling.** Game log says "Connor Duch", leaders sheet says "Connor Dutch", [[Master Roster]] says "Conner Duch". Used the roster spelling.
- **Corey assist.** The Aug 25 assist is credited to "N/L Corey" (No. 7/12), meaning the scorer did not know whether it was Noah or Lucas. Still unresolved.
- **Oct 22 opponent.** Spreadsheet is the first record of Marquette as the district opponent. Added to the schedule note.
