## Talking points

- **Stradling for #1 GK.** Tied for the district lead in shutouts (7) and fewest goals allowed (32), on a schedule with twice as many TC games as Marquette. 1.06 goals against per game outside those four games. 4 shutouts against district teams. Marquette's keeper faced the TC schools twice; Jace faced them four times and still matched him.
- **Back line.** 5 goals allowed in 8 games against the district field, with 4 shutouts, and every district opponent held to half its season rate or less. Cotant-Harrington, N. Corey, L. Corey and Dunn were in front of Jace for that.
- **Context for the TC games.** 14 goals against in 4 games sounds like a lot until you see Gaylord's 21, Alpena's 18 and Marquette's 11 in only 2.
