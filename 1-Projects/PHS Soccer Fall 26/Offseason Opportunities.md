# Offseason Opportunities

Winter and spring options to hand out to the [[Boys Soccer Fall 26]] players after the season. One printable flier per opportunity.

## PYSA Spring Premier Lakers Tryouts

- **When:** Sunday, Nov 8, 2026, 11:00 AM - 12:30 PM
- **Where:** Click Road Soccer Complex, Petoskey
- **Who:** Boys 2008-2012 birth years (U13-U19)
- **Cost:** Free, but players must be registered to try out
- **Register:** https://petoskeysoccer.sprocketsports.com/all-programs/category/756
- **Details:** https://petoskeysoccer.sprocketsports.com/club/premier
- Spring league play in MSPSP (Michigan State Premier Soccer Program), roughly 10-team division, 4-5 home and 4-5 away games, at least two travel weekends (mostly Metro Detroit). Autumn Blast and Fresh Coast Classic tournaments. Training 2-3x weekly, 75-90 min. Merit-based playing time. PYSA says a winter training option is also planned.
- Flier: [[2026-11-08 PYSA Premier Lakers Tryouts - Flier.pdf]]

## Midwest United FC North Tryouts

- **When:** Nov 8, 2026. U14 and older 11:00 AM - 12:30 PM, U7-U13 12:30 PM - 1:30 PM
- **Where:** Boyne Mountain Sports Complex
- **Who:** Boys or girls looking for a winter/spring team or program
- **Info:** www.midwestunitednorth.com
- Note: the U14+ session overlaps the PYSA tryout exactly (same day, same 11:00-12:30 slot). Players cannot attend both.
- Flier (printed as received): [[2026-11-08 Midwest United North Tryouts - Flier.pdf]]
