Reconciliation on 2026-09-24 against MHSAA. Everything below is a change from the sportsYou-derived version:

- **Thu Oct 8 (TC Central): Away, not Home.** MHSAA has it at Coast Guard Fields, Traverse City, 6:45. This reverses the earlier call that the sportsYou "V @ TC Central" title was a typo. It was not.
- **Added:** Fri Sept 18 vs Harbor Springs, home, 7:00. Was missing from the table entirely.
- **Added:** Tue Oct 20 and Thu Oct 22 district games. Opponent, site and time not yet published.

- **Tue Oct 20 district vs Alpena: 6:00.** Won 2-1 in OT. Per MHSAA, 2026-10-25.
- **Removed:** Sat Aug 22, the presumed day 2 of the Boyne invitational. MHSAA shows all three invitational games on Fri Aug 21 (2:00 vs Hartford, 4:00 vs Grass Lake, 6:00 vs Grand Rapids South Christian). The "AWAY @ Boyne" entry on the Aug 22 facility grid did not correspond to a contest.
- **Thu Sept 24 (Cadillac): varsity only.** No JV game; the standing 5:00 JV slot was dropped from the row.

- **Tue Oct 6 @ TC West:** site is 5376 N. Long Lake Rd, Traverse City. TCW wants our roster in advance; gate $7 cash / $6 card, GoFan preferred. See [[2026-09-30 TCW Athletic Events 10-5 to 10-11]].

- **Aug 21, third game** is listed by MHSAA at Click Rd. Soccer Complex in Petoskey, not at Boyne Mountain. Worth a look if the invitational is scheduled the same way next year.
